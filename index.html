How to compress and crop an image

  1. Choose an image, drop it on the page, or paste it from your clipboard.
  2. If it is sideways, use Rotate and flip. Then drag the crop box or its handles to frame what you want. Pick a shape such as 1:1 or 16:9 if you need an exact ratio.
  3. Choose a format and quality. Or tick Limit file size and enter a target such as 100 KB. The panel at the top shows how much smaller the file is.
  4. Open Preview to check the result, then download it.

What it can do

  • Crop freely or lock the box to a ratio: 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9 and their portrait versions.
  • Rotate by 90 degrees and flip horizontally or vertically.
  • Compress JPEG and WebP with a quality slider, or convert to PNG when you need lossless output.
  • Hit a target file size. The tool looks for the highest quality that fits, and shrinks the pixel size only when quality alone is not enough.
  • Resize by longest side, from 3840 px down to 640 px.
  • Process many images at once and download them as one ZIP with the batch compressor.

Your images stay on your device

Everything happens inside your browser. Your photo is never sent to a server, so it is safe to use with personal photos and documents, and you do not spend time waiting for an upload.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many images you process.

Which image types can I open?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF and AVIF, depending on what your browser supports. Some browsers cannot open HEIC photos from iPhones, so convert those to JPEG first.

Will the quality drop?

JPEG and WebP are lossy, so a lower quality setting means a smaller file with slightly less detail. Settings between 70 and 85 look almost identical to the original for most photos. Use Preview with Actual size to check before you download.

Why is my PNG not getting smaller?

PNG is lossless, so the quality slider does not apply. To shrink it, reduce the longest side, or switch to WebP or JPEG. Keep PNG for graphics with sharp edges or transparency.

Which format gives the smallest file?

WebP is usually the smallest. JPEG is accepted almost everywhere, which makes it the safe choice for upload forms.

My photo is sideways. Can I fix it?

Yes. Use Rotate left or Rotate right in the side panel. The crop box turns with the image.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. You can drag the crop box and its corners with your finger.
